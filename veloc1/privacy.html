Privacy Policy – Veloc1

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Veloc1 is an offline single-player game published by MDsoftware ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. The game has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not request internet access.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (coins, cleared missions and best scores) in a local file on your device. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its storage.

Children

Because we collect no data, the game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
