Veloc1

Arcade space shooter for Android

Pilot your ship through 40 missions of vertical-scrolling combat. Dodge bullet patterns, collect coins and power-ups, defeat a boss at the end of every mission, and spend your earnings on weapon and ship upgrades.

  • 40 missions plus bonus levels, each with a unique boss
  • Four primary weapons and secondary weapons: missiles and bombs
  • Short sessions of 3–10 minutes, one-finger touch controls
  • Fully offline. No ads, no accounts, no data collection
