Pixel art ticket-writing game for Android

You are a police officer walking one street in a Polish town, from the late 1990s to 2002. Inspect the parked cars, spot what is wrong and write the right ticket before the shift ends. A wrong ticket counts against you.

  • 40 shifts, each with its own goal
  • Parking offences, broken lights, worn tyres, expired registration and more
  • Later shifts add the tow truck, wanted cars, warnings, audits and night patrols with a flashlight
  • Short sessions of a few minutes, landscape touch controls
  • English and Polish
  • Shifts 1–10 are free. A one-time “Full game” purchase unlocks shifts 11–40
  • Playable offline. No ads, no subscriptions, no accounts

Citation to gra pixel art na Androida: patrolujesz jedną ulicę, kontrolujesz auta i wystawiasz prawidłowe mandaty. 40 służb, po polsku i po angielsku. Służby 1–10 są darmowe, a jednorazowy zakup „Pełna gra” odblokowuje służby 11–40. Bez reklam i subskrypcji.
